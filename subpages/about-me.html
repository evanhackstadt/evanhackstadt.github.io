About me

Curiosity in and out of the lab.

My interests and values go far beyond research.

Thinking in systems, with people in mind.

My interests have always moved between technology, systems, and the world around us. I'm drawn to cutting-edge technology, but care deeply that it be used for good.

Biology, medicine, public health, and ecology are the domains that give my work meaning and impact.

Outside of research, I’m drawn to fitness and nutrition, plant-based eating, travel and urbanism, reading, and writing. Those interests keep me attentive to the contexts that data alone cannot capture.

I care about environmental sustainability, social justice, and public health—and I hope to build a career that has a meaningful, practical impact.
